A Simple Soup

Peel two carrots, one onion and three potatoes, and cut everything into small even pieces. Warm a spoonful of oil in a heavy pot and soften the onion slowly, without letting it brown. Add the carrots and potatoes, stir for a minute, then cover with water and a pinch of salt.

Let the soup simmer for about twenty minutes, until the potatoes fall apart when pressed with a spoon. Taste it, add pepper and a little butter, and serve with dark bread. The next day the flavour is deeper, so it is worth making a large pot.
